## Prototype Review

Device:
iPhone Safari

Review URL:
Use the exact verified PR-numbered GitHub Pages URL from the workflow result.

Review Steps:

1. Open the exact HTTPS URL in the `Prototype Review` section of the Compass report or PR handoff.
2. Confirm the first screen shows `第四のページ` and `灯りの先に、世界がひらく。`.
3. Observe the world without touching it and notice that the observatory already has a quiet breathing rhythm.
4. Touch the foreground water or lower part of the stone path once.
5. Watch for the refined Prototype 3 answer: a small light leaves the observatory, drifts toward the touched place, and disappears into the existing rhythm.
6. As soon as that response has settled, touch a different foreground place within two seconds.
7. Confirm that no reply light or local bloom appears and that the mist, water, observatory, and small lights continue without interruption.
8. Wait about four more seconds, then touch once again.
9. Confirm that the same destination-first answer is available after the next world breath begins.

Review Points:

- Did the world feel alive before the first touch?
- When the second touch received no answer, did the world still feel alive rather than frozen or broken?
- Did the continuing ambient rhythm make the silence feel intentional?
- Did silence strengthen the sense that the world has its own time?
- Did the later return of the same answer feel coherent rather than like a delayed reward?
- Did the absence of feedback create calm curiosity, or did it create confusion and distrust?
- Did the timing tempt repeated tapping or make the experience feel like a hidden game mechanic?
- Did the experience preserve the depth, near-future invitation, and parent trust of refined Prototype 3?

Expected Feeling:

> 「触れても、世界には世界の時間がある。」

Expected result:

- the complete refined Prototype 3 world appears without login, download, or setup
- ambient mist, water, and small lights remain quiet and compositionally stable
- the first eligible touch receives the existing curved destination-first answer
- a touch during the remainder of the same eight-second breath receives no reply light, bloom, message, or substitute feedback
- the world never pauses, resets, flashes, or advertises that it chose silence
- after the next breath begins, the unchanged Prototype 3 answer is available again
- no interaction creates navigation, sound, reward, progress, instruction, pressure, or stored state
